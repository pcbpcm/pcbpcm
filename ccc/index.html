CCC Exam 2026

Course on Computer Concepts – NIELIT

💻 CCC Exam evaluates basic computer knowledge and skills for government and private sector opportunities.
📅 Program Overview
  • Course: Course on Computer Concepts (CCC)
  • Conducted by: NIELIT (National Institute of Electronics & Information Technology)
  • Mode: Online / Offline Examination
  • Duration: 100 minutes (approx)
📄 Eligibility Criteria
  • Anyone who has passed or appeared in 10th / 12th or equivalent can apply
  • No age limit
  • Basic knowledge of computers is recommended
📚 Syllabus
  • Introduction to Computers and IT
  • Operating System & Word Processing
  • Spreadsheet, Presentation, Internet & Email
  • Cyber Security & Basic Networking
📝 Exam Pattern
  • Type: Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ)
  • Total Questions: 100
  • Marks: 100 (1 mark per question)
  • Duration: 2 hours
  • Qualifying Marks: 50%
